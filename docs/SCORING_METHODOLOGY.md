# Scoring Methodology

Suitability score = weighted combination of:
- urgency fit: 30%
- service-line fit: 20%
- travel feasibility: 15%
- readiness: 15%
- verification/availability: 15%
- freshness/contactability: 5%

Hard exclusion rules:
- candidate not verified when verification is required
- candidate service line mismatch
- candidate travel time above slot ceiling
- candidate earliest date after slot date

All values are operational heuristics, not clinical decisions.
